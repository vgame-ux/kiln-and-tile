Privacy Policy

Kiln & Tile for Android · Developer: Veldenix · Effective date: 13 November 2026

Kiln & Tile is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. This policy explains what information the game uses, what Google’s advertising tools in the game collect, and the choices you have.

At a glance

  • We (Veldenix) do not receive your personal information. Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google’s software in the game collects some device and usage data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and sends it to Google.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google’s consent form asks for your choice first. You can change it later in the game under Pause → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or stop all collection by uninstalling the game.

1. Who we are

Kiln & Tile is developed and published on Google Play by Veldenix (“we”, “us”). This policy applies to the Kiln & Tile app for Android, version 1.0.0 and later, and to this website.

2. Information kept on your device

To work properly, the game saves the following only on your device, in the game’s private app storage:

  • your unfinished game (board, waiting pieces and score), so you can carry on later;
  • your best score;
  • your settings: sound on or off, vibration on or off, and whether you have finished the tutorial;
  • simple counters that space out ads, such as how many games have been played and when an ad was last shown.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else by the game. We cannot see it.

Android backup: if you have turned on Android’s backup in your device settings, Android may include the game’s saved progress and settings in your device backup, stored in your Google account under Google’s terms. We cannot access these backups.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Kiln & Tile is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google. The game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Android version 25.5.0) to show ads.

Data collected and shared with Google

According to Google’s disclosure for this SDK, it automatically collects and shares the following with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

DataWhat it includes
IP addressYour device’s internet address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location. The game itself has no access to your precise location.
App and ad interactionsFor example app launches, taps and ad video views.
Diagnostic informationFor example app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.
Device and account identifiersThe Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google may use this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see summary reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown.

The ads you will see

  • Rewarded ads (optional): once per game you can choose to watch a short ad for a new set of pieces (“Fresh Set”), and once per game, when you run out of room, to clear three rows and keep playing. A reward is given only for an ad you chose to watch to the end.
  • Interstitial ads: occasionally, between games only, never during a game or during the tutorial.

The game does not show banner ads and has no in-app purchases.

4. Consent and privacy options

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK version 4.0.0) to manage privacy choices for ads. Each time the game starts, it checks with Google whether a consent message is required for you. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before ads are requested, and ads are requested only when your choices allow it.

  • Your choices are stored on your device and passed to Google with ad requests, so ads respect them.
  • Where a privacy options entry point is required, you can review or change your choices at any time in the game: open the menu (top-right button) to reach the Pause screen, then tap Privacy options.
  • If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, as described in Google’s policies.

5. Android permissions

The game asks for no permissions that need your approval. The installed app includes these normal permissions, most of them added by Google’s ads software:

PermissionUsed for
Internet and network stateLoading ads and Google’s consent message. The game itself plays without a connection.
Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Android ad servicesAdvertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention by Google, within the choices you make.
VibrateGentle vibration when you play. You can turn it off under Pause → Vibration.
Wake lock and foreground serviceAdded by Android support libraries used by Google’s ads software for background tasks. The game itself does not run a foreground service.

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, phone, messages or notifications.

6. What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details, and has no account or sign-in.
  • It does not include analytics or crash-reporting services of our own. Unity’s analytics and diagnostics services are switched off.
  • It does not include in-app purchases or take payment information.
  • It does not let players chat or share content with each other.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Information on your device is kept until you delete it. Uninstalling the game removes it, and so does Android Settings → Apps → Kiln & Tile → Storage → Clear storage. Clearing storage also resets your best score, settings and saved consent choices.
  • We do not hold personal information about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete.
  • Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted by Google under its own policies; see How Google retains data we collect. You can limit its use with the choices in section 8.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Consent: change your ad privacy choices in the game under Pause → Privacy options (shown where required).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings (on most devices Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Deleting it stops apps from using it for personalised advertising.
  • Google ad settings: manage ad personalisation for your Google account at My Ad Center.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or under US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google through the links above. You are always welcome to contact us; we will reply and help where we can.

9. Children

Kiln & Tile is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

10. Security

Saved games stay in the game’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Google’s ads software sends data to Google encrypted in transit (TLS).

11. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, analytics or ads, and loads no third-party content. As the host, GitHub may record technical information such as visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security and operations; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because the game adds a feature or Google changes its ads software, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
