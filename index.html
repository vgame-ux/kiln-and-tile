Kiln & Tile

A calm 8×8 block puzzle in glazed ceramic. Place pieces, clear rows and columns, and turn each piece once. No timer, no rush.

Coming soon to Google Play

Simple to learn

Drag a piece onto the board. Fill a row or column to clear it. A three-move tutorial shows the way.

One turn per piece

Tap a piece to turn it before you place it. Each piece can be turned once, so plan the turn.

Made to feel calm

Hand-glazed colours, soft ceramic sounds and gentle vibration, all of which can be switched off.

Picks up where you left off

Your game saves automatically on your device. No account or sign-in needed.

Free, with fair ads

Kiln & Tile is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always your choice. Other ads appear only between games, never during play. Read the privacy policy to see what data the ads use and how to manage your choices.
